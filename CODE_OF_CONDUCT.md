# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Engineering Notes is a technical community. It works best when discussion is precise, respectful and focused on the work. Everyone who takes part, as a reader, contributor, reviewer or maintainer, commits to making participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Scope

This code applies in every space the organisation runs: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, comment threads on the articles, community chats and events. It also applies when someone represents Engineering Notes in public, for example when posting from an official account.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique code and claims, never people.
- Back technical claims with evidence: a source, a specification or a reproducible run.
- Assume good intent, and ask clarifying questions before drawing conclusions.
- Accept correction gracefully, including from people with less experience.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Publishing anyone's private information, such as a home address or email, without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately off-topic content.
- Plagiarism, or presenting generated or copied work as your own original research.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers at **mwangijoenjoroge@gmail.com** with the subject line `Conduct report`. Include links or screenshots where you can. Reports are handled confidentially, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide what action to take, guided by the impact of the behaviour:

| Step | When | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | Unprofessional or unwelcome behaviour, once | A private, written explanation of what was wrong. A public apology may be requested |
| 2. Warning | A single serious incident, or repeated minor ones | A warning with stated consequences, and no interaction with the people involved for a set period |
| 3. Temporary ban | A serious breach, or continued behaviour after a warning | No interaction with the community for a set period |
| 4. Permanent ban | A pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards anyone | Permanent removal from all community spaces |

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests and discussions that do not follow this code. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce it in good faith may be removed from their role.
