# Governance

## Roles

| Role | Who | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Founder and lead maintainer | [Joseph Mwangi](https://github.com/JosephNjorog) | Direction of the organisation, final say on content and releases, organisation settings and security |
| Maintainers | Members of the `@Engineering-Notes/maintainers` team | Review and merge pull requests, triage issues, keep CI green, publish corrections |
| Contributors | Anyone with a merged contribution | Bug reports, corrections, experiments and code |

## Decisions

- Day-to-day decisions, such as merging a pull request, are made by a maintainer after review and passing CI.
- Technical disagreements are settled with evidence: a source, a specification or a reproducible run.
- Decisions that affect the whole organisation, such as a new series, a new repository, a licence change or a new maintainer, are proposed in Discussions and decided by the lead maintainer after at least seven days for comment.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have made several accepted contributions, review others' work constructively, and follow the code of conduct may be invited to join the maintainers team. Maintainers who are inactive for six months may be moved to emeritus status, with thanks.

## Repositories and content

- Each article series has its own repository. Each article has one folder with its own tests.
- Articles are published on [codemwangi.substack.com](https://codemwangi.substack.com). The code in this organisation is what the articles' numbers come from.
- Content and code are released under the MIT License unless a repository states otherwise.

## Changes to this document

Changes to governance are proposed by pull request to the `.github` repository and announced in Discussions.
